# Sign in

**Sign in with browser** opens the Overleaf login page in a separate, temporary browser window. Log in exactly as you normally do (Google, ORCID, your university's SSO, or email and password). The window closes by itself once you are in.

Prefer not to? Choose **Paste a session cookie**: in a browser where you are logged in to Overleaf, open DevTools → Application → Cookies and copy the value of `overleaf_session2`.

Using a self-hosted Overleaf? Run **Overleaf: Use a Different Overleaf Server…** first.

Your session is stored in VS Code's secret storage. It is never sent anywhere except to your Overleaf server.
