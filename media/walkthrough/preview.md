## Compile and preview the PDF

Right-click a project and choose **Open PDF Preview**, or click the preview button at the top of any open `.tex` file.

- **Recompile** compiles on Overleaf with the project's own compiler and main document. While the preview is open it also recompiles by itself when the project changes (your saves, collaborators' edits, AI edits). Change this under the arrow next to Recompile, or with the `overleafMcp.pdf.autoCompile` setting.
- **Errors and warnings** appear in the toolbar and in the Problems panel; click one to open the line.
- **Double-click the PDF** to jump to the source. In the editor, right-click and choose **Show in PDF** to find a line in the PDF.
- Draft mode, stop on first error, zoom, download and the raw log are in the toolbar.
